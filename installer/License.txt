Strata Historian ODBC Driver

This driver reads PI data through PI Web API. It is read-only: it never writes to PI.

Licensing of PI access is your responsibility. AVEVA's terms may require a PI System Access (PSA) licence for a non-AVEVA program that reads PI data. Confirm this with whoever manages your AVEVA agreement before using the driver on production data. See docs\team-rollout.md.

Test builds. Builds that are not signed by your organization are for testing only, not for gateways or production hosts.
